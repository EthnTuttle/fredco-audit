Key Findings

The top 10 owners account for $1.5M (15.5%) of all delinquent taxes despite representing less than 0.4% of accounts. The $5,000–$10,000 bracket — 779 accounts — drives 68% of total dollars owed. Corporate entities (LLCs, corporations, trusts) hold 598 delinquent parcels totaling $2.3M. This report is updated daily by the county; payments made after the report date may not be reflected.

How This Works

Delinquent tax parcel IDs were matched against Frederick County's GIS parcel database (50,005 parcels). 96.7% of records matched, adding district, zoning, and assessed value to each delinquent account. The enriched table delinquent_with_gis is available in the Playground.

About This Data

This report is published daily by the Frederick County Commissioner of the Revenue. It lists real estate accounts with outstanding balances as of the report date. Payments made after the report is generated will not be reflected until the next day's update. For the most current status of a specific parcel, use the county's Real Estate Public Inquiry portal.
